Try KnockOff's comparison journey

Interactive demonstration using fixed sample observations. Select a product to see how KnockOff separates advertised prices, conditional offers and eligibility. No retailer websites are contacted and no current prices are implied.

An observed price difference is not a guaranteed saving or proof of eligibility for a price beat. Delivery and stock must be confirmed with the retailer. This demonstration does not use affiliate links.

How KnockOff evaluates an offer

1 · EXACT PRODUCT

Identity first

Compare brand, manufacturer model, variant and GTIN when available. Similar names do not establish identical products.

2 · EVIDENCE

Price type matters

Keep advertised prices separate from interactive Price Check results, manual quotes and checkout prices.

3 · BUYING PATH

Retailer confirmation

Show the appropriate next step, with clear conditions and no unsupported savings guarantee.

Select an offer’s verification button to see its buying guidance.

What the customer does next

1Find an exact product match using brand, model and variant, where available.
2Compare advertised and conditional offer types separately, including delivery charges.
3Open the retailer's own page and activate Price Check or request a quote when required.
4Confirm price, stock, eligibility and checkout total directly with the retailer.
